# Horsehead Homestead

Welcome to the repository for **Horsehead Homestead**, a digital porch for a family of 9 (plus 2 dogs and countless chickens) documenting our beautiful, chaotic homesteading and homeschooling life!

## About Us

From the kitchen table to the woodshed, life at Horsehead Homestead is never quiet. We're building a space where we can share our favorite disasters, triumphs, and everything in between.

Here is a sneak peek at what's brewing:

*   **🐓 Chickens & Dogs:** Chasing heritage breeds, gathering breakfast, and keeping the pups out of the coop (usually).
*   **🌱 Gardens & Fairy Magic:** Sustaining a family of 9 with homegrown veggies while building miniature enchanted worlds in the roots.
*   **📚 Homeschool & Games:** Where lessons turn into custom-made board games and recess consists of epic backyard trick shot challenges.
*   **🪵 Wood, Sweat & Tears:** Chopping, stacking, and prepping for winter. It's the ultimate gym membership, and everyone's invited.
*   **🥖 Baking & Cooking:** Mass-producing sourdough, experimental wood-fired recipes, and feeding a small army three times a day.
*   **🤪 The Antics:** With 7 kids, if something isn't currently breaking, flying through the air, or laughing hysterically, check the pulse.

## Development

This is a simple static website built with HTML and CSS.

To view the site locally, just open `index.html` in your web browser.
